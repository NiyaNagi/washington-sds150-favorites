# Uniden SDS150: step by step

The scanner gets Favorites Lists, not a memory list. The installer writes them
straight into Sentinel's workspace (profile **Preset**), and you then write the
scanner from Sentinel. There are 39 lists, each named to fit the scanner's
18-character display:

| Quick keys | Lists |
|---|---|
| 1-6 | NM Public Safety, NM Tactical, NM Air, NM Ham, NM Rail & Marine, NM Business & GMRS |
| 10-18 | PS King County, PS Snohomish, PS Pierce, PS South Sound, PS Kitsap/Olympic, PS North Sound, PS Eastern WA, PS Statewide, PS Encrypted |
| 20-26 | OUT Wildfire, OUT West Mountains, OUT East Mountains, OUT WA Safety All, OUT Upper Lena, OUT Lake Ozette, OUT Weather/SAR |
| 30-33 | AIR Civil & ATC, AIR SAR & Medevac, MIL Air & Ground, MED EMS & Hospital |
| 40-44 | MAR Marine & USCG, MAR Ferries & VTS, RAIL Freight Rail, TRAN Transit, TRAN Roads & WSDOT |
| 50-54 | HAM Repeaters, HAM DMR Networks, HAM ARES & Nets, HAM Simplex & Sats, HAM HF Nets |
| 60-62 | BIZ GMRS FRS MURS, BIZ Business/Util, BIZ Events & Media |

Every list is installed **monitored**, and its quick key switches it in and
out of the sweep while scanning - type the key and press `E/YES`. Monitor is
the gate, not the switch: Uniden's *Select Lists to Monitor* decides which
lists a quick key can reach at all, and "if the item is set to No, it cannot
be turned on for scanning using quick keys" (SDS150 manual, p.29). A list
installed with Monitor off answers its own quick key with a double beep, which
is what the 34 category lists used to do.

The one exception is **PS Encrypted**, left behind the gate deliberately:
several hundred talkgroups that cost sweep time and produce no audio.

What each Near Me list holds is in
[Updating every radio: In the car](../fleet-updates.md#in-the-car-sds150).

**You need:** Uniden BCDx36HP Sentinel, the scanner's USB cable, and the GPS
connected when you test.

## Files

| Path | What it is |
|---|---|
| `radio-data\sds150\exports\hpe\` | One `.hpe` per list, named by its key (`PS-KING.hpe`); this is what gets installed |
| `radio-data\sds150\exports\favorites-overview.md` | Every list, what it holds and which catalog lists it merges |
| `radio-data\sds150\backups\sentinel-workspace\` | A zip of the Sentinel workspace, taken before every install |
| `%USERPROFILE%\Documents\Uniden\BCDx36HP` | Sentinel's workspace (the install target) |

## Steps

1. **Close Sentinel completely.** The installer will not run while Sentinel has
   the workspace open.
2. **Install the lists.** In `Update Radios.cmd`, tick only the SDS150, tick
   **Write to the radios**, and press **Update selected**. From a terminal:

   ```powershell
   .venv\Scripts\wasds150.exe --home .wasds150-home fleet update --radios sds150 --execute
   ```

   It exports, runs a dry run, backs up the workspace, installs every list
   into profile Preset and verifies the result. Lists an earlier install wrote
   under catalog keys (`FL01 - WA SAR & Mutual Aid`, `KC29 - Redmond Local`)
   are removed, because a category now holds them. Lists you made yourself are
   left alone. If anything fails, it puts the backup back.
3. **Set the scanner profile.** The installer never writes `profile.cfg`, so
   check these once, either in Sentinel for profile Preset or on the scanner
   after writing:
   - **Service Types: all on.** On the scanner, press FUNCTION then ZIP/SERVICES
     and turn each type on with E/YES. If Aircraft, Business, Railroad, Other,
     Interop, Federal or Military is off, NM Air, NM Business & GMRS and most of
     NM Rail & Marine stay silent.
   - **Select Lists to Monitor: Full Database off.** The scan is then the Near
     Me lists.
   - **WX Operation:** Program SAME = King County **053033**, Weather Alert =
     Named FIPS Code, WX Alt Priority = On.
   - **Close Call:** Set CC Mode = **CC DND**.
4. **Reopen Sentinel**, open profile Preset and spot-check a few lists. NM
   Public Safety is first and BIZ Events & Media is last.
5. **Write the scanner.** Connect the SDS150 by USB and write from Sentinel.

## Check on the scanner

- With GPS connected, the scanner starts on the three Near Me lists that are on
  at startup and nothing else.
- FUNCTION + ZIP/SERVICES shows every service type on.
- A tower or ground frequency near home is audible on NM Air.
- A category quick key (for example 10 for PS King County) adds that list to the scan.

## Undo

Close Sentinel. Take the newest zip in
`radio-data\sds150\backups\sentinel-workspace\` and extract it over
`%USERPROFILE%\Documents\Uniden\BCDx36HP`. Then write the scanner again.
