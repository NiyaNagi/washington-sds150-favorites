# Upper Lena Lake listening profile

Upper Lena Lake is at the Olympic National Park campsite coordinate
**47.6337317, -123.2096262**, taken directly from the park's public Camp Areas
GPX. The approach begins in Olympic National Forest's Hood Canal area and
enters Olympic National Park. NPS describes the route as 7.3 miles, rising from
about 700 feet to 4,550 feet, moderate-to-strenuous, with snow possible in any
season. Overnight permits are limited year-round.

Radio reception is never guaranteed in steep Olympic terrain. A scanner is a
receive-only situational-awareness tool—not an emergency communicator. Carry a
satellite messenger/PLB, map/navigation tools and the NPS 10 Essentials, leave a
trip plan, and check current trail, fire and weather conditions.

## Generated lists

### UL00 — Upper Lena - Fast Local Essentials

Compact, deterministic, no private database required. It includes:

- Olympic National Park/Forest published conventional channels
- WA SAR, WSP District 8 conventional, REDNET
- Mason Sheriff/Fire and Jefferson Sheriff/Brinnon Fire/Olympic Ambulance
- WA DNR main/common and national wildfire air guard/flight-following/ICP
- civil and military guard, SAR air, Airlift NW and HEAR
- Hood Canal/Puget Sound marine distress, bridge, liaison, VTS and working channels
- Capitol Peak, Puget Sound Marine and Seattle NOAA Weather transmitters
- Mason County ARC 146.720 MHz repeater (PL 103.5) and national ham calling channels
- NWAC FRS channel 7

This is the recommended trail/campsite list when scan-cycle speed matters.

### UL01 — Upper Lena - Wilderness Essentials

Broad public wilderness pack. It combines UL00 with statewide interoperability,
DNR/NIFC, Olympic park/forest, aviation, marine, medical, amateur, personal
radio, road crews and weather. It is intentionally comprehensive; disable
unneeded departments if scan cycles become too long.

### UL02 — Upper Lena - Regional Public Safety

Requires the local Sentinel HPDB for complete P25 content. It combines WSP,
WSDOT, Thurston/Mason, Kitsap/Jefferson, Clallam and Olympic park/forest
profiles. Encrypted law groups can be avoided; fire/EMS, transportation and
interop are the practical focus.

### UL03 — Upper Lena - Complete Area

Combines UL01 and UL02 into one convenience list. With the current local HPDB it
contains the complete verified component set. Use location control and disable
distant departments for practical scan speed.

Enable only one of UL00/UL01/UL03 at a time. They intentionally overlap, and
simultaneously scanning a derived profile plus its source lists repeats work and
slows the chance of returning to an active local channel.

All copied departments receive an Upper Lena circular location tag: 45 miles
for UL00/UL01 and 85 miles for UL02/UL03. Original site coordinates remain
available for trunked-system site selection.
