# Puget Sound net schedule

Researched **2026-09-12** from sponsoring clubs' and repeater operators' own
pages. RepeaterBook and RadioReference were used to cross-check a frequency or
tone, never as the sole source for a schedule, following the source hierarchy
in [Puget Sound ham repeaters and nets](puget-sound-ham.md).

The repeaters below are the `Nets` block: one zone and one scan list on every
radio that has room, each channel carrying its day and time in the note and its
transmit offset, so they are channels to check into rather than only listen to.

Times are Pacific. **Recheck a schedule before relying on it** - clubs move nets
and rarely date their pages.

## Not confirmed, and deliberately not programmed

- **LWHC Friday Round Robin** and **Kirkland ARES/RACES** - both listed on
  K7LWH 145.490 / 441.075, which the club says is in storage after the Rose
  Hill site was vacated. Current home unknown.
- **Redmond ARES weekly check-in** - the club confirms a net but withholds the
  frequency and tone publicly; email info@redmond-ares.org.
- **Greater Kingston Radio Club** - Mike & Key says Sunday 19:00, Kitsap County
  ARC says Tuesday 19:00. No club website to settle it.
- **Marysville & Tulalip ACS** - the city page's "447.700 (in 442.700)" looks
  transposed, and part of the net rotates among channels announced on air.
- **Seattle ACS SE Sector, NW Sector and Multi-Repeater nets** - on the ACS
  calendar, but with recurrence rules that expired in 2022-2023.
- **North Bend ARES 441.775 and SECAST 444.925** - no club source publishes a
  tone, so none was guessed.
- **No Microsoft or Overlake club exists.** The Redmond-area groups are Radio
  Club of Redmond and Redmond ARES.

## Known conflicts

- **146.925 Snohomish ACS tone.** WA7DEM, the sponsoring agency, says 156.7.
  Mike & Key and an undated WA7LAW page say 123.0. The catalog uses 156.7 and
  says so in the note; worth confirming on air.
- **147.080 Cougar Mtn callsign.** Mike & Key and the Sea-Tac Repeater
  Association call it WW7STR, ARES of King County calls it W7WWI, and WWATS
  calls their machine WW7ATS. All agree on 147.080 + / 103.5.
